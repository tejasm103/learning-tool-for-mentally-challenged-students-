Piece Guide

♙ Pawn: Moves 1 square forward, captures diagonally.
♖ Rook: Moves any distance horizontally or vertically.
♘ Knight: Jumps in an 'L' shape (2 squares then 1).
♗ Bishop: Moves any distance diagonally.
♕ Queen: Moves any distance in all directions.
♔ King: Moves 1 square in any direction. Protect him!
