🛑 Safe & Unsafe Boundaries

🤝

Safe Touch

High-fives, holding a parent's hand across the street, or a warm hug from family that makes you feel safe and comfortable.

✋

Saying "NO!"

If any touch makes you feel uncomfortable, confused, or scared, you have the right to say "NO!" in a loud, firm voice.

🗣️

Tell a Trusted Adult

Always talk to your parents, school teachers, or school nurse. Never keep secrets that make you feel worried or unsafe.
