Facial Expressions & Feelings

💬
“Expressions can give us clues, but asking kindly is the best way to understand how someone feels.”
😊

Happiness

👀 Facial Clues: Smiling mouth, lifted cheeks, warm sparkling eyes.

🌟 Example Situation: When you finish a fun puzzle or share a snack with a friend.

🤝 Kind Response: Smile back or say: I am glad you are happy!
